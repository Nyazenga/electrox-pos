## 🎯 The Problem

Zimbabwe's retail sector — particularly electronics, hardware, and general retail — is dominated by **manual record-keeping**, **Excel spreadsheets**, and **disconnected systems** that lead to:

- **Stock shrinkage & theft** — businesses lose up to 15–20% of stock annually due to poor tracking
- **Tax non-compliance** — ZIMRA's new virtual fiscalization mandate (FDMS) requires digital receipt submission, yet most small retailers have no system to comply
- **No real-time visibility** — owners with multiple branches have no way to see what's happening across locations in real time
- **Lost revenue** — manual invoicing, no credit sales tracking, no laybye management means money slips through the cracks

**There is no affordable, ZIMRA-compliant, Zimbabwean-built POS system designed specifically for SME retailers.**

## ✅ What's Already Built (Current Product Status)

The platform is **fully functional and deployed in production** with the following modules:

### Core Business Operations
- **Point of Sale (POS)** — Fast, touch-friendly sales interface with shift management, cash drawer tracking, and real-time receipt printing
- **Inventory Management** — Stock levels, stock takes, Goods Received Notes (GRN), inter-branch stock transfers, low stock alerts
- **Product Management** — Categories with flexible characteristics, barcode generation & scanning, specific item tracking (serial numbers, IMEI, color, storage for electronics)
- **Invoicing System** — Professional invoices with customizable templates, terms & conditions, PDF generation, and email delivery

### Financial & Sales Management
- **Credit Sales & Account Management** — Track customer accounts, balances, and payment histories
- **Laybye System** — Manage lay-by purchases with deposit tracking and completion workflow
- **Trade-In Module** — Accept trade-ins with AI-powered valuation suggestions
- **Refunds, Credit Notes & Debit Notes** — Full returns workflow with stock restoration

### Compliance & Reporting
- **ZIMRA Fiscalization (FDMS)** — Automatic receipt submission, QR code verification, fiscal day management, credit/debit note submission — **fully certified for production**
- **69+ Report Types** — Sales by period, by user, by category, tax reports, profit margins, end-of-day printouts, deleted receipts audit trail, customer purchase histories
- **Multi-Currency** — Full USD and ZWL support with exchange rate management

### Platform & Security
- **Multi-Tenant SaaS Architecture** — Each business gets isolated data with shared infrastructure (low cost to scale)
- **Role-Based Access Control** — Granular permissions (40+ permission keys) for Administrators, Managers, Cashiers, etc.
- **Multi-Branch Support** — Unlimited branches per tenant with real-time data sync
- **RESTful API** — Full API with Swagger documentation for third-party integrations
- **AI Integration** — Google Gemini AI for trade-in valuations and business insights
